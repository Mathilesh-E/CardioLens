Methodology & Dataset Facts

Formal documentation of dataset provenance, preprocessing, model training, calibration strategy, and safety guidelines.

System Architecture & Pipeline Provenance

Dataset: CDC Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS 2015), 253,680 survey records across 21 features plus target (HeartDiseaseorAttack, 9.42% positive prevalence).

Sampling & Splitting: Stratified 15,000-row representative cohort partitioned into 70% Train (10,500), 15% Validation (2,250), and 15% Test (2,250), preserving exact 9.42% class balance across all subsets.

Model Suite: Primary LightGBM with cost-sensitive weighting (scale_pos_weight=9.62) and validation early stopping, alongside XGBoost, Random Forest (class_weight balanced), and Standardized Logistic Regression.

Calibration: Prefit isotonic regression applied on validation set to transform raw margin outputs into true empirical posterior probabilities (Brier score loss: ~0.067).

Uncertainty: 30-member bootstrap ensemble generating 5th-95th percentile risk confidence intervals.

Crucial Safety & Data Limitations
1. Observational Survey Data: All data is observational from 2015. Projections represent statistical associations, NOT clinical guarantees.
2. Self-Reported Responses: Diagnoses of hypertension, diabetes, and smoking status are self-reported by survey respondents.
3. Educational Research Prototype: PulseIQ is strictly an educational research tool and does not replace medical advice from a licensed physician.
